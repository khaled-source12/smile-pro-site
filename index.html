What is SMILE Pro?

SMILE Pro is a flapless laser vision correction procedure performed with the ZEISS VisuMax 800. A femtosecond laser shapes a thin lenticule of tissue inside the intact cornea, which is removed through a micro-incision under 2mm. No corneal flap is created. The laser runs for about 8 seconds per eye and the full procedure takes around 15 minutes for both eyes.

No flap. Ever.

SMILE Pro uses a 2mm micro-incision invisible to the naked eye - there's no corneal flap involved at any stage. This minimally-invasive approach is a key reason it's well suited to active lifestyles.

Flapless · minimally invasive

Egypt has one of the world's highest dry eye rates. SMILE Pro's micro-incision preserves the corneal nerves that control tear production, which is associated with a clinically lower dry eye incidence.

What is the difference between SMILE Pro and LASIK?

LASIK requires creating a corneal flap. SMILE Pro removes tissue through a micro-incision under 2mm with no flap at all, which preserves more corneal nerves and biomechanical strength. In practice that means a minimally invasive procedure well suited to active lifestyles, with clinically lower rates of post-operative dry eye and a laser time of 8 seconds per eye instead of 30 to 60.
