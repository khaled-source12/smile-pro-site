Why SMILE Pro

LASIK was the best option
until SMILE Pro existed

No flap. Ever.

LASIK creates a large corneal flap that never fully heals - one impact years later can dislodge it. SMILE Pro uses a 2mm micro-incision invisible to the naked eye. No flap means no flap complications, for life.

Zero flap risk · permanent

Drive yourself home the same day

The ZEISS laser operates for 8 seconds per eye. The entire procedure takes 15 minutes. Most patients are back at their desk the same day. No hospital stay, no general anaesthesia.

Most patients drive the same day

Your eyes won't feel like sandpaper

Egypt has one of the world's highest dry eye rates - and LASIK makes it significantly worse. SMILE Pro's micro-incision preserves the corneal nerves controlling tear production. Clinically proven lower dry eye incidence.

Critical for Egyptian patients
