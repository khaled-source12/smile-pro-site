Why SMILE Pro

Engineered for Precision and Comfort

SMILE Pro utilizes the ZEISS VisuMax 800 to reshape corneal tissue internally without creating a standard flap. This design prioritizes structural integrity and rapid recovery.

No Flap Creation

By avoiding a traditional corneal flap, SMILE Pro maintains maximum corneal stability and reduces post-operative sensitivity.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Numbing eye drops are administered before starting, eliminating sharp sensation. You may feel mild pressure on the eye for about 8 seconds during laser application. Post-procedure sensation is typically minimal, with slight watery feeling for a few hours.
The ZEISS VisuMax 800 laser operates for approximately 8 seconds per eye. Total room time, including preparation and alignment, takes around 15 minutes for both eyes.
Candidates are generally adults aged 18 to 55 with stable prescription over the past year and suitable corneal thickness/topography. Final candidacy is determined through comprehensive diagnostic scanning.
The EGP 48,000 package includes the procedure on both eyes, all surgical consumables, post-op medicated eye drops, and 12 months of follow-up evaluation appointments.
Most patients resume screen reading, general desk work, and driving within 24 hours. Non-contact sports can be resumed within a day, while swimming is cleared after 1 week.
