Before you compare prices, read this

Because the technology is different. PRK uses a surface laser with no flap creation. LASIK adds a microkeratome flap. Femto-LASIK replaces the blade with a femtosecond laser. SMILE Pro eliminates the flap entirely using a 2mm keyhole incision on the ZEISS VisuMax 800. Each step up adds precision, safety, and faster recovery — but also cost. The right question isn't "which is cheapest" but "which is safest for my specific cornea."

A Pentacam is a rotating camera that maps your entire cornea in 3D — measuring thickness, curvature, and elevation at thousands of points. It tells us definitively which technologies are safe for your specific eyes and which are not. Without it, any price estimate is just a guess. That's why we start every patient journey with one.
