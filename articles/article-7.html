SMILE Pro vs. Contoura LASIK: Which Is Right for You?

Both **SMILE Pro** and **Contoura LASIK** represent the pinnacle of modern laser vision correction. While both procedures achieve exceptional visual outcomes, their underlying surgical mechanisms and suitability profiles differ significantly.

Quick Comparison Overview

Feature SMILE Pro Contoura LASIK
Corneal Flap Flapless (Micro-incision ~2mm) Requires Corneal Flap Creation
Laser Time Under 10 Seconds 20 - 40 Seconds
Dry Eye Risk Minimal to None Temporary Potential Risk
Corneal Integrity Maximum Structural Strength Retained Dependent on Residual Bed Thickness

Which Technique Fits Your Eyes Best?

**SMILE Pro** is often the top recommendation for active individuals, athletes, patients prone to dry eye, or those with thinner corneas due to its keyhole approach. **Contoura LASIK** excels at mapping unique corneal topographies for custom corrections.
