<p>Thanks to its minimally invasive keyhole technique, <strong>SMILE Pro</strong> offers one of the fastest post-operative recovery periods in modern laser refractive surgery. Here is what to expect during your visual recovery journey.</p>

<h2>Post-Operative Recovery Timeline</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>First 4–6 Hours:</strong> Mild tearing or scratchiness may occur. Resting with your eyes closed is recommended.</li>
<li><strong>Next Day:</strong> Most patients achieve 80%–90% vision improvement and can resume driving and light daily tasks.</li>
<li><strong>Within 1 Week:</strong> Full return to desk work, screen time, and light exercise routines.</li>
<li><strong>Within 1 Month:</strong> Complete visual stabilization and optimal night vision clarity.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Essential Do's and Don'ts After Surgery</h2>
<ul>
<li>Use prescribed anti-inflammatory and lubricating eye drops as scheduled by Dr. Mohamed El Naggar.</li>
<li>Avoid rubbing or pressing on your eyes for at least one week.</li>
<li>Keep tap water and soap directly out of your eyes for 3 days post-op.</li>
<li>Refrain from swimming in pools or natural water bodies for two weeks.</li>
</ul>
