<p>Both <strong>SMILE Pro</strong> and <strong>Contoura LASIK</strong> represent the pinnacle of modern laser vision correction. While both procedures achieve exceptional visual outcomes, their underlying surgical mechanisms and suitability profiles differ significantly.</p>

<h2>Quick Comparison Overview</h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Feature</th>
<th>SMILE Pro</th>
<th>Contoura LASIK</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>Corneal Flap</strong></td>
<td>Flapless (Micro-incision ~2mm)</td>
<td>Requires Corneal Flap Creation</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Laser Time</strong></td>
<td>Under 10 Seconds</td>
<td>20 - 40 Seconds</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Dry Eye Risk</strong></td>
<td>Minimal to None</td>
<td>Temporary Potential Risk</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Maximum Structural Strength Retained</td>
<td>Dependent on Residual Bed Thickness</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Which Technique Fits Your Eyes Best?</h2>
<p><strong>SMILE Pro</strong> is often the top recommendation for active individuals, athletes, patients prone to dry eye, or those with thinner corneas due to its keyhole approach. <strong>Contoura LASIK</strong> excels at mapping unique corneal topographies for custom corrections.</p>
