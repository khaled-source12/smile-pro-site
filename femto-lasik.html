Why Femto LASIK

Engineered for Accuracy & Safety

Unlike traditional LASIK which utilizes a mechanical blade (microkeratome), Femto LASIK uses infrared laser energy to form an ultra-thin, uniform corneal flap with micro-level predictability.

100% Blade-Free Safety

Eliminates blade-related risks by using microscopic laser pulses to construct a custom-contoured corneal flap.

Micro-Precision

Customized Edge Architecture

The laser creates bevelled flap edges that interlock securely back into place, promoting rapid structural healing.

Optimal Healing

Versatile Correction Range

Highly effective for wide ranges of short-sightedness (myopia), long-sightedness (hyperopia), and astigmatism.

Broad Applicability
Surgical Process

How Femto LASIK Works

01
Stage 1 — Femtosecond Flap Creation

Sub-Micron Laser Flap

The computer-guided femtosecond laser sends thousands of light pulses per second beneath the corneal surface, creating a protective thin flap in about 10–12 seconds.

02
Stage 2 — Excimer Refractive Reshaping

Personalized Vision Correction

The flap is gently lifted, and an excimer laser reshapes the inner corneal curves according to your exact optical map to correct focal errors.

03
Stage 3 — Natural Flap Repositioning

Instant Flap Adhesion

The flap is smoothed back into place where it natural adheres without stitches within minutes, serving as a biological bandage for rapid comfort.
